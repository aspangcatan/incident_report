## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, reliable, and modern institutional clinical aesthetic tailored for critical hospital governance, incident reporting, and safety operations within Philippine public healthcare facilities. 

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Anchors public accountability, legal integrity, and clinical precision without appearing bureaucratic or dated.
- **Urgent yet Calming:** Mitigates the cognitive panic of clinical errors and adverse drug events through structured, unhurried information architecture.
- **Hygienic & Meticulous:** Employs pristine, deliberate surfaces that mirror clinical sanitation and operational efficiency.
- **Accessible & Resilient:** Engineered for high-throughput institutional workstations, triage terminals, and nurses' stations with varied lighting conditions and diverse operator tech fluency.

### Visual Design Movement
A structured fusion of **Modern Corporate SaaS** and **Clinical Minimalism**. The system relies on clear structural hierarchies, high-contrast borders, refined surface elevation, and strict semantic color coding to communicate life-critical incident telemetry.

## Colors

The color system delivers high-contrast visibility meeting WCAG 2.1 AA+ standards across all clinical monitors, administrative terminals, and emergency tablets.

### Primary Palette
- **Deep Clinical Navy (`#1E3A8A`):** Anchors critical navigation, primary system actions, structural headers, and institutional authority.
- **Deep Slate Canvas (`#0F172A`):** Used for commanding typography, primary structural chrome, and high-impact micro-states.

### Secondary & Accent Palette
- **Clinical Teal (`#0D9488` / Accent `#14B8A6`):** Communicates operational progress, nursing notes, clinical triage paths, and tertiary confirmations.
- **Information Sky (`#0284C7`):** Marks telemetry notices, procedural tooltips, and protocol citations.

### Functional & Triage Alert Spectrum
- **Emergency Crimson (`#DC2626`):** Sentinel events, adverse drug reactions, patient deaths, and critical equipment collapses. Must be used with high-contrast text surfaces.
- **Warning Orange (`#EA580C`):** Near-miss events, high-risk non-compliance, and severe security violations.
- **Controlled Clinical Amber (`#D97706`):** Pending committee reviews, unverified witness statements, and SLA timeouts.
- **Verified Forest Green (`#16A34A`):** Root-cause analysis resolved, administrative sign-offs completed, and closed cases.

### Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for base page backgrounds.
- **Sub-Surface Tier:** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) for structured toolbars, tabular headers, and nested canvas sections.
- **Active Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` (Pristine White) for card modules, clinical forms, and dialog surfaces.
- **Structural Outlines:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for standard division, transitioning to `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) for interactive control borders.

## Typography

The typographical pairing serves distinct roles: **Plus Jakarta Sans** lends institutional credibility, structured authority, and legibility to page headers, modal titles, and section dividers. **Inter** handles high-density data sheets, clinical narratives, audit timestamps, and operational metadata.

### Tabular Data Alignment
All data grids, time elapsed indicators, case reference IDs (e.g., `INC-2025-0891`), and patient vital metrics must enforce OpenType tabular figures (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`). This prevents character shifting when monitoring real-time incident queues.

### Hierarchy & Legibility Controls
- Headings maintain strict negative tracking to preserve structural authority across multi-line incident titles.
- Section titles paired with government seals or institutional division badges must use `title-sm` with upper tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.05em`) for categorical clarity.
- Micro-labels inside status badges and priority tags require medium or semi-bold weights to avoid stroke degradation on non-Retina institutional hospital terminals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt base grid with a 12-column fluid grid system, built to maximize dense operational information without cognitive clutter.

### Breakpoints & Layout Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column grid. Standard navigation rail fixed at 280px on the left canvas, yielding high vertical real estate for incident logs, multi-pane triage workflows, and root cause analysis panels. Gutters sit at `gutter` (1.5rem) with an outer margin of `margin` (2rem).
- **Tablet / Clinical Cart (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout. The primary navigation rail collapses into an iconified docking drawer (72px). Gutters shift to 1.25rem; side margins contract to 1.5rem. Case review sidebars transform into layered off-canvas sheets.
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout. Total margin contracts to `margin-mobile` (1rem) and `gutter-mobile` (1rem). Dense split tables reflow into stacked priority status cards with thumb-accessible trigger targets.

### Spacing Usage Rules
- `space-xs` (0.25rem): Inline spacing between badge icons and label text; micro-gaps within input button groupings.
- `space-sm` (0.5rem): Spacing between form labels and inputs; vertical spacing between metadata label-value pairs.
- `space-md` (1rem): Default padding for table cells, inline action groups, filter chips, and card inner contents.
- `space-lg` (1.5rem): Module separations, card component internal padding for master dashboards, and section dividers.
- `space-xl` (2rem): Major narrative blocks, multi-step incident stepper divisions, and institutional summary container gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids expressive or neon shadows, adopting **Tonal Layering** balanced by **Soft Clinical Ambient Shadows** tinted with deep slate navy (`rgb(15, 23, 42)`).

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Level 0):** Pure slate background (`#F8FAFC`). Flat, un-elevated, structural foundation.
2. **Sub-Containers (Level 0.5):** Recessed panels (`#F1F5F9`) with a 1px continuous border (`#E2E8F0`). Used for filtering toolbars, table headers, and audit trails.
3. **Primary Cards & Grids (Level 1):** Solid white cards (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and a diffused micro-shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
4. **Dropdowns, Menus & Tooltips (Level 2):** Elevated interactive components:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06);`
   - Paired with `#CBD5E1` edge-definition borders.
5. **Modal Overlays & Critical Alert Prompts (Level 3):** Incident escalation modals, legal attestation dialogs, and emergency lockouts:
   - `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08);`
   - Underlaid with an institutional backdrop scrim: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.60)` accompanied by `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry, balancing professional clinical authority with ergonomic modern software aesthetics.

### Geometric Allocations
- **Base Elements (`rounded`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Standard buttons, text field inputs, dropdown toggles, and table action rows.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Incident summaries, patient triage containers, investigation modules, and statistical cards.
- **Major Enclosures (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Global modal frames, slide-out case review drawers, and hero alert banners.
- **Status Pills & Micro Indicators (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Severity status chips, incident stage indicators, and user role avatars.

- **Secondary Clinical:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E3A8A`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#1E3A8A`.

- **Dimensions:** Default height 40px (desktop), 44px (touch/tablet); padding `0 1rem`; typography `label-md`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Designed with high-contrast text on pastel-tinted structural grounds:
  - **Emergency (Sentinel):** Ground `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #F87171`.
  - **Warning (Major Adverse):** Ground `#FFF7ED`, text `#9A3412`, border `1px solid #FDBA74`.
  - **Pending Review:** Ground `#FEFCE8`, text `#854D0E`, border `1px solid #FDE047`.
  - **Closed/Resolved:** Ground `#F0FDF4`, text `#166534`, border `1px solid #86EFAC`.
  - **Telemetry Info:** Ground `#F0F9FF`, text `#075985`, border `1px solid #7DD3FC`.
- All badges use `rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, accompanied by an active 6px tinted indicator dot.

### Incident Lifecycle Progress Trackers
- Horizontal stepper component spanning 5 sequential hospital governance phases: *Reported -> Triage Assessment -> Committee Investigation -> Corrective Action -> Verified Closed*.
- Completed steps display `#16A34A` lines with check icon nodes; active steps feature a dual-ring `#1E3A8A` beacon with an animated pulse ring; future steps remain `#E2E8F0` with neutral text (`#64748B`).

### Form Inputs & Textareas
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Roundedness: `0.5rem`.
- Padding: `0.625rem 0.875rem`. Label above input using `label-md` with neutral slate color (`#334155`).
- Focus state: Border color `#1E3A8A`, subtle outer glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.15)`).
- Error state: Border `#DC2626`, focus glow `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.15)`, error message typography `body-sm` in `#DC2626`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Control size: 18px x 18px. Border: `1.5px solid #64748B`.
- Checked state: `#1E3A8A` fill with white check/dot icon.
- Focus outline: 2px offset ring in `#93C5FD`. Touch targets enforced at a minimum 40px bounding box via negative margins.

### Cards & Analytical Containers
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Roundedness: `rounded-lg` (1rem).
- Header structure: Integrated border-bottom `1px solid #F1F5F9`, padding `1rem 1.5rem`, housing `title-md` text and contextual status badges.
- Card Body: Padding `1.5rem`.

### Structured Metadata Tables
- Headers: `#F8FAFC` background, text `#475569`, typography `label-sm`, uppercase tracking, border-bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Rows: `#FFFFFF` ground, alternating with `#FBFDFF` for long-scroll incident logs. Hover state `#F1F5F9`. Border bottom `1px solid #F1F5F9`.
- Cell contents use `body-md` with `tnum` numeric values.

### Institutional Seals & Identity Badges
- Standardized header placement for hospital division accreditation and Department of Health tracking stamps. Sealed containers utilize an inset double border (`border: 1px solid #E2E8F0; outline: 1px solid #F1F5F9; outline-offset: -3px;`) with monochrome navy or secondary teal SVG vector emblems.
